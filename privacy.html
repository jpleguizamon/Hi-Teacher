Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10 · updated October 3, 2026

In a nutshell

Hi Teacher is an app for teachers to organize their lessons and for students (or their parents or guardians) to keep up with them. We store only what's needed for that to work. A student's data is seen by their teacher and by the people linked to that student in the app. We don't sell or share data with anyone for advertising.

Who decides who gets in

Each teacher has a code. The student or parent/guardian uses that code to ask to join, and the teacher decides whom to accept. The teacher is responsible for their students' data in the app.

For anyone under 18, the request needs permission from a legal guardian.

What data we store

  • Profile details: name, phone number, date of birth, instrument and, if there is one, the parent or guardian's name and phone number.
  • Lessons: days and times, attendance and absences, make-up lessons, what was covered, and homework.
  • Payments: monthly fees and charges (paid or unpaid). The app doesn't store card or bank details.
  • Assessments: grades for each lesson; the student sees them only if the teacher chooses to show them.
  • Teacher's notes: notes about the student. Only the teacher sees them.
  • What the student records in the app: practice time, practice journal, songs, goals, and messages and requests to the teacher.
  • Notifications: if the person turns them on, the device's technical address for receiving notifications.

Who sees it

  • The teacher sees their students' data.
  • The student and the parent/guardian see only what belongs to that student (lessons, payments, notices, and whatever the teacher shares).
  • No student sees another student's data.

Where it's stored and for how long

The data is stored on Supabase, a cloud database service, protected by sign-in and by rules that let each person see only what's theirs. A copy is kept on the device so the app opens quickly and works offline.

Data is kept for as long as the account exists. When someone deletes their account, what they created in the app is deleted right away. The lesson and payment records the teacher keeps stay with the teacher until they delete them (the student can ask for this through the app).

Recordings and notifications

Audio and video recordings made in the student app stay on the device only. They aren't sent to the cloud or to the teacher unless the person shares them.

Notifications are optional. They only arrive if the person turns them on, and they can be turned off at any time. Notification text never shows amounts, grades, or reasons.

Your rights (LGPD, Brazil's General Data Protection Law)

  • See and download your data: in the student app, Profile → "Download my data". Teachers use Settings → Data and backup → "Export data".
  • Correct it: in Profile, "Request a change".
  • Delete it: in Profile, "Delete my account" (student or parent/guardian) or Settings → Account → "Delete my account and all data" (teacher). You can also ask the teacher to delete their records.
  • Withdraw consent: just delete your account.
